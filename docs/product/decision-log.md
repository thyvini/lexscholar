# Product decisions and authority

## Standing authority

The owner sets product direction and approves feature boundaries. Sage may
answer product questions from approved sources, citing the relevant source,
and may make reversible, low-impact decisions within an approved boundary.
Record delegated choices with the rationale and affected requirement; do not
present a recommendation or an inference as owner approval.

Escalate new scope, privacy or retention commitments, constitution changes,
acceptance of risk, and release publication to the owner. When authority or
impact is unclear, ask rather than treating silence as approval. Group
non-urgent questions with options, impact and the criteria or tasks blocked.
Keep independent, already-authorized work moving.

Feature-level approval authorizes routine implementation and integration of
task PRs within that boundary, subject to the repository's review and CI gates.
It does not authorize publication to `main`, a release tag, or a GitHub release.
It does not override `AGENTS.md` or the constitution.

## Decision record

Record substantive delegated decisions here as they arise. Include date,
decision maker, decision, rationale, the approved source or feature boundary,
affected requirements, and any owner decision needed. A proposed decision must
be labelled as such until the appropriate authority approves it.
